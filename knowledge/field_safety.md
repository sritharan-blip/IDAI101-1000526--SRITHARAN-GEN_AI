# Field safety and source use

## Disease identification limits

One photograph is not enough to confirm a crop disease. Similar symptoms can have different causes, including pests, nutrient stress, water stress, and weather. Describe visible signs as possibilities and recommend a local extension service or plant-health laboratory for consequential decisions.

## Pesticide and treatment safety

Pesticide approvals, crop labels, protective equipment, and permitted uses vary by location. Do not invent a product, dose, or application interval. A farmer must follow the locally registered product label and applicable rules, and consult a qualified local adviser. Start with monitoring and locally appropriate non-chemical controls where practical.

## Seasonal and location-specific guidance

Crop suitability, irrigation timing, soil amendments, and pest pressure depend on local conditions and the crop. Do not infer a local recommendation from a broad regional average. State uncertainty and refer farmers to their local agriculture extension service.

Use the official local source for current, location-specific guidance:
